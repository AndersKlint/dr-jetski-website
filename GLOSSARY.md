# Dr. Jetski Studios

Dr. Jetski Studios is the home for an independent developer's software and creative work, serving both software users and people evaluating that work professionally.

## Language

**Project**:
A distinct piece of work presented by the studio, including an app, plugin, game, command-line tool, developer package, or desktop extension. A project may be available to users or still in development.

**Featured project**:
A project selected for prominent presentation on the homepage. Featured projects have an explicit editorial priority, independent of their release maturity.

**Availability**:
Whether a visitor can currently obtain or try a project, and through which public destinations. Availability is separate from development maturity: a finished private package can be unavailable, while an alpha or beta can be downloadable.

**Looping preview**:
A short, silent video that plays inline and repeats while visible, showing a project in motion without sound.

**Demo**:
A visitor-controlled video that may include audio and never plays automatically when a project page loads.
