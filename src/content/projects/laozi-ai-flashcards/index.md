LaoZi AI Flashcards is a spaced-repetition app focused on pragmatic, productive Chinese study, with possible expansion to other languages. AI generates detailed flashcards, descriptions, and example sentences from inputs such as a photograph of a textbook vocabulary list.

It targets serious learners, including university language students.

- AI-generated flashcards with descriptions and example sentences.
- Photo input, for example textbook vocabulary lists.
- Spaced-repetition scheduling.

Platform availability: Android, Linux, Windows, and web are described as platforms, but only the Android alpha APK is confirmed ready, and its public link is still pending.
